## What we collect and why

- **Phone number** — to log you in with a one-time code and to send your ticket by SMS.
- **Name, e-mail (optional)** — for your profile and to e-mail your ticket.
- **Passenger details** — name, gender, age or date of birth, and where required NID or passport
  number and expiry. Needed by bus operators, Bangladesh Railway and airlines to issue a ticket.
- **Bookings and payments** — what you booked, amounts, payment status and refunds. We never see
  or store card numbers or wallet PINs: you enter them on the payment gateway's page.
- **Push token** — if you allow notifications, to send booking updates.
- **Price alerts and saved travellers** — only what you choose to save.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos or advertising ID, and the app has no ads or
analytics trackers.

## Who receives your data

Only what is needed for the booking you make: the transport operator (passenger details for that
trip), the payment gateway (amount, booking reference), the SMS / e-mail / push providers (the
message to you). We don't sell personal data.

## How we protect it

HTTPS for all traffic; NID and passport numbers encrypted in our database; login codes and tokens
stored only as hashes; on your phone, your session is encrypted with Android Keystore and tickets
are kept in app-private storage excluded from backups.

## How long we keep it

Account data while your account exists. Booking and payment records for [N] years, as required for
accounting and dispute handling. Login codes expire after 5 minutes.

## Your choices

- Edit your profile and saved travellers in the app.
- **Delete your account**: Account → Delete account. Personal data is removed; booking records we
  must keep by law are kept without your profile, for the period above.
- Questions or requests: **[privacy e-mail]**.

## Children

The app is not for children under 13. Children may travel as passengers added by an adult.
